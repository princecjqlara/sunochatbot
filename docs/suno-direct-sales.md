# Suno direct sales and earnings

The Owner supplied Suno's actual policy on 20 November 2026: direct sales, staff earn 20% of song sales, 50% of tips and 20% of revision fees. The implemented payment basis is each verified payment, including deposits, allocated to the Makata explicitly assigned to the sale. This replaces the inherited Veo earnings selection and Closed/Done workflow in Suno. It does not change the retained chatbot.

For a PHP 500 song payment, PHP 100 tip and PHP 200 paid revision fee, staff earn PHP 100 + PHP 50 + PHP 40 = **PHP 190**. The company share is PHP 610 before other costs. Commission is not a payroll payout or a profit calculation.

Sales records hold a contact, Page, assigned Makata and currency. A song charge and each extra revision charge have their own price and outstanding balance. Adding an unpaid charge does not recognize revenue or earnings. A tip is a separate payment and cannot settle a song or revision balance. Receipt references and actual Philippine payment times are required; staff must attest that they checked the evidence. The system does not verify a bank transfer independently or infer payment from a tag, chat message, photo or AI prediction.

The backend fixes the rates; clients cannot supply a commission percentage. Payment writes lock the sale, check the current workspace/branch/Page, and preserve idempotent request receipts. Overpayments and over-refunds are rejected. Verified refunds reference an original payment and reverse the corresponding 20% or 50% earnings. Cumulative rounding within a charge/tip stream conserves minor units through split receipts, refunds and repayment. Currency totals remain separate. Changing a conversation assignment does not reassign a recorded sale or its prior earnings.

Sales & payments, Earnings, the sales calendar, dashboard and staff earnings share the authoritative `suno_sales_report`. Totals use actual payment dates; the sales book lists orders and their receipt history across dates. Contact profiles and drawers link to the same scoped ledger. Legacy workspace links to deals, Closed/Done, receipts or quota-driven finance open Suno's direct-sales workspace. The old financial API routes reject writes, inherited quota/incentive workers remain paused, and the compatibility salary RPC uses Suno's actual payment ledger and rates. Automatic discipline derived from Veo quotas is disabled; manually authorized staff controls remain.

The additive migration is `202611200101_suno_direct_sales.sql`. It preserves customer records, queues, chatbot settings and historical compatibility tables. No fictional employee, receipt, sale or fee is seeded. Private backups, rollback preflight, the retained-chatbot source check and the full release gate are required for deployment. Internal `veo_*` identifiers retained for compatibility do not define Suno's business policy.

## Song work and revisions

Migration `202611200102_suno_sales_song_work.sql` links each direct sale to a song-work record. Open **Song work & revisions** to track Working, Revision requested or Reported sent. These are staff declarations; none confirms client receipt, verifies a payment or creates earnings. A revision request does not automatically charge the customer. Agreed revision fees and verified payments remain separate entries in Sales.

The initial work assignee is the sale's assigned Makata. An authorized supervisor can reassign current song work within their permitted branch and Pages. Work reassignment keeps the original sale payee and prior earnings unchanged. The work list follows current work assignment; the financial report follows the recorded payee's branch. Each update has a reason, current version and immutable history. A stale save returns a conflict so a second user cannot overwrite a newer change.

The existing Telegram `/songs`, `SONG SENT <UUID>` and `SONG REVISION <UUID>` commands accept the new Suno sale ID. Group, Page, verified Telegram identity and current song assignment are checked before a declaration and again before the normal outbox renders its response. The earlier order-based protocol remains available for retained records. No new Telegram sender or schedule is created. The CRM adapter is sufficient; the Python command format is unchanged.

Supervisors' sale and work assignee selectors use the complete live branch directory rather than the personal-profile directory. Disabled accounts are excluded from new assignment choices. API routes are `GET/POST /api/workspace/song-work`; direct RPC/table access remains private. Work reads do not expose receipt amounts to a new assignee in another financial branch.
